# Grand Theft Auto: every feature

## 1. Moving on foot

| ID | Feature | What the player does or sees | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| GTA-01-001 | Walking | Push the left stick (or WASD) to walk; the character steps at a slow default pace. | V, Online, IV, SA, VC |
| GTA-01-002 | Running | Push the stick fully to jog faster than walking. | V, Online, IV, SA, VC |
| GTA-01-003 | Sprinting | Hold the sprint button (Shift / X or A rhythm tap in old games) to run at top speed. | V, Online, IV, SA, VC |
| GTA-01-004 | Stamina stat | Sprinting drains a stamina bar (hidden); low stamina shortens how long you can sprint. | V, Online, SA |
| GTA-01-005 | Jumping | Press the jump button (Space / X / A) to hop over kerbs and small gaps. | V, Online, IV, SA, VC |
| GTA-01-006 | Vaulting obstacles | Running at a low wall, fence or rail makes the character climb or vault over it. | V, Online, IV |
| GTA-01-007 | Ladder climbing | Walk into a ladder to climb it up or down. | SA, VC |
| GTA-01-008 | Surface swimming | Enter water and swim on the surface; a stamina/oxygen drain applies. | V, Online, IV, SA |
| GTA-01-009 | Underwater diving | Press the dive button to swim below the surface and explore the seabed. | V, Online, SA |
| GTA-01-010 | Breath/oxygen meter | A blue air bar drains while submerged and refills at the surface. | V, Online, SA |
| GTA-01-011 | Drowning | Stay under with an empty air bar and health drains until death. | V, Online, SA |
| GTA-01-012 | Scuba gear and rebreather | Equip a scuba tank to breathe underwater freely, sold at Ammu-Nation in Online. | V, Online |
| GTA-01-013 | Cover system | Press the cover button (Q / R1) near an object to snap to it, and move along or peek out. | V, Online, IV |
| GTA-01-014 | Blind fire from cover | Shoot without peeking, exposing only a hand and weapon over the cover. | V, Online, IV |
| GTA-01-015 | Stealth mode | Click the stick (or press Ctrl) to crouch and move quietly, lowering noise and detection. | V, Online, SA |
| GTA-01-016 | Combat roll | Press the jump/roll button while aiming to dive sideways and dodge bullets. | V, Online |
| GTA-01-017 | Ragdoll physics | Characters flop and tumble realistically when hit by cars, explosions or bullets. | V, Online, IV |
| GTA-01-018 | Fall damage and limping | Landing from a big drop hurts or kills; after heavy damage the character limps until healed. | V, Online, IV |
| GTA-01-019 | First-person mode | Toggle first-person view to walk, aim, drive and shoot from the character's eyes. | V (PS4/Xbox One/PC), Online (PS4/Xbox One/PC) |
| GTA-01-020 | Climbing steep slopes | On rough terrain the character scrambles up inclines that would otherwise block movement. | V, Online, SA |
