# M14 — The whole city builder

From: `docs/plan/features/mechanics.md` GAP-01-001 to GAP-01-068 (rooftop parachuting,
GAP-01-055, is M17's); `docs/plan/CAPABILITIES.md` S2, S8, S9, S10's roundabouts, S24,
S25, S31, S33, S37, S40, S42, S43, S53 and S58; world-scale plank 2 (overpasses and
underpasses, `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-19-world-scale-design.md`); D2 changed (curves
are no longer "after the sell check"). When it closes, the player builds a city with every
tool Cities: Skylines gives: any road at any angle, highways, tunnels and bridges, every
density and specialisation, industry chains, park and campus areas, policies, landmarks,
tourism, decoration, terraforming, map tiles and scenarios. Pillars: build it, live in it;
the city lives; every tool is expressive.

Needs first: M5 (the tool frame, roads, road types, bulldoze, budget, milestones,
overlays), M3-4 (ops and undo), M4-2 (river), M4-3 (terrain), M12 (utilities), M3-6 (routed
traffic). Lane: city.

**Every new road, area and building is still generated where the player puts it**
(`AGENTS.md`): the player's acts are ops on the map (M3-4), and frontage, lots and street
furniture along them are planned from the map, never hand-placed.

## Keys

City view only (the street is untouched):

- **Drag** lays a road, as in M5; **click on the road's middle while dragging** bends it;
  **Shift while dragging** keeps it straight.
- **S** cycles what the tool snaps to: grid, road ends and lengths, 45° and 90°, building
  fronts, nothing. (S in the city view moves no one: the street's WASD are off there.)
- **Holding right mouse and moving it** turns a building being placed; a right click
  without moving still puts the tool down (M5-7).
- **Page Up and Page Down** lay the next road one level up or down (elevated, ground,
  tunnel). **U** shows the underground view.
- Every new tool sits in the city view's tool bar; no other new key.
