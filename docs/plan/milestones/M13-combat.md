# M13 — Combat

From: D9 (the operator, 2026-10-04: combat "just like GTA, Watch Dogs and Cyberpunk");
`docs/plan/features/mechanics.md` GAP-08-001 to GAP-08-066 (the three vehicle weapons,
GAP-08-005, -006 and -030, are M18's); `docs/plan/CAPABILITIES.md` G10, G11, C19, W27 and
the weapons of C11. When it closes, the player fights with fists, melee weapons, guns and
throwables, takes cover, sneaks and takes people down, gets hurt, heals, dies and comes
back, and the city answers every shot. Pillars: the player feels capable; consequence fits
the act; every tool is expressive.

Needs first: M10-3 (collision; the bodies and boxes a bullet hits), M10-4 (walkers react),
M10-10 (police on foot), M2-2 (rigged people and the walker pool), M6-1 (aim at a thing),
M6-3 (Focus), M7-1 (sound), M7-4 (settings), M7-8 (gamepad), M11-4 (money, safehouse),
M5-5 (the clinic). Lane: combat.

**The look holds** (`AGENTS.md`): real-world weapon kinds with invented makers' names,
no neon tracers, no holographic sights, blood kept small and dark. A Cyberpunk "smart" or
"tech" weapon keeps its play in a modern form (M13-3, M13-23).

**Draw cost.** The player's weapon 1, every other person's weapon 1 (one instanced pool),
muzzle flashes, tracers and casings 1, bullet holes, scorch and blood 1 (one decal pool),
thrown things in flight 1, fireballs 1; smoke and gas share M10.T9's smoke pool. A fight
costs at most 6 more draws; the HUD is DOM, 0 draws.

## Keys

On foot (rebindable on M7.T11's screen; the pad per M7-8):

- **Right mouse, hold:** aim, over the shoulder; with a scoped weapon, the scope.
  **Left mouse:** fire, or strike with a melee weapon or fists; hold for a heavy strike.
  Right mouse with a melee weapon or fists blocks.
- **R** reloads. **G** throws the equipped throwable; hold **G** to detonate thrown sticky
  bombs. **Tab, hold:** the weapon wheel. The mouse wheel changes weapon only while
  aiming; otherwise it still moves the camera.
- **C** crouches; crouching within 1 m of a wall, car or low cover takes cover there.
  **Space while aiming** rolls; Space alone still jumps (M10-9).
- **E** uses what is in front (`ROADMAP.md`, "One key, one meaning"): behind an unaware
  person while crouched it grabs them; then tap E knocks them out, hold E for 1 s
  kills. At a downed body, E picks it up; at a dumpster or an open car boot, E drops it in.
  At a dropped weapon, E takes it.
- **X** uses a medkit. **Caps Lock** spends a full adrenaline bar (M13-21). **Shift
  while scoped** holds the breath. **Q** is still Focus; with a gun aimed it is the
  slow aim of M13-21.

In a car: right mouse aims a sidearm out of the window, left mouse fires (M13-20).

## Decisions for the operator

- **Animation source.** CMU's mocap has walks, falls and boxing but few aiming,
  reloading or cover poses. M13.T13 poses them by script on the rig unless the operator
  approves an outside library (Mixamo needs an Adobe account and its terms).
- **Age rating.** Guns, blood and killing make it a mature game; Steam's content survey
  (M9) asks.
