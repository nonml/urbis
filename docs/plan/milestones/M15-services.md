# M15 — Every city service

From: `docs/plan/features/mechanics.md` GAP-02-001 to GAP-02-031; `docs/plan/CAPABILITIES.md`
S13, S14, S17, S18, S19, S20, S23, S28, S32, S44, S45's heating and snow clearing, S49,
S51, S54, S55, S56 and S57; D12's rest (hospital, fires that spread). When it closes, the
city runs every service Cities: Skylines has: every kind of power plant and water works,
the whole waste chain, hospitals, deathcare, schools to university, post, telecom,
heating, road upkeep, loans, service budgets, people who are born, age and die, rent and
homelessness, and disasters the city must warn, shelter and rebuild after. In the street
the player sees each one at work. Pillars: the city lives; build it, live in it.

Needs first: M5-5 (services with catchments), M5-6 (budget), M12 (power, water, garbage,
fire, fleets), M3-6 (routed traffic), M10's crash physics, M13 (explosions), M14-4
(the underground view). Lane: city.

## Keys

None. Every new building is a tool in the city view's tool bar (M5), and every new view
is an overlay in M5-8's list. In the street, the emergency number is a contact in M27's
phone.
